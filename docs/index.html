How the estimate works

YouTube MP4 files with H.264 video and AAC sound use roughly these amounts of space per minute. Calm videos sit at the low end and busy ones at the high end:

QualityResolutionPer minute10 minutes
1080p1920 x 108015 to 30 MB150 to 300 MB
720p1280 x 7208 to 13 MB80 to 130 MB
480p854 x 4804 to 6 MB40 to 60 MB
360p640 x 3602 to 3 MB20 to 30 MB

Real files can land a little outside these ranges, because every upload is encoded differently. A downloader that reads the actual stream sizes from YouTube will show the exact number.

Which quality should you pick?

On a phone, 720p looks almost as sharp as 1080p at about half the size. Use 1080p for a TV, a laptop or editing. On a tight data plan, 480p or 360p keeps a 10 minute video under 60 MB.
